Desalination Division

Global Pioneers in Reverse Osmosis Systems

We design and operate state-of-the-art reverse osmosis seawater treatment infrastructure, restoring critical access to dry urban regions.

By replacing outdated evaporative thermal methods with high-pressure semi-permeable membrane modules, our systems consume up to 80% less electricity, establishing a model for carbon-optimized seawater desalination. Over 500 million cubic meters of drinking water are synthesized yearly to preserve natural riverbeds and vulnerable underground reservoirs.

Pre-Filtering Membranes

Highly effective sand filtration arrays and cartridge membranes shield osmosis modules from biological blockages.

Energy Recovery Devices

Utilizes isobaric chambers to recycle up to 98% of the mechanical energy from the reject brine outflow.

Remineralization Post-Phase

Ensures output water exceeds all WHO sanitary indices using pure CO2 injection over calcium limestone bases.

Coastal Outfall Ecology

Brine dispersal occurs via high-velocity Venturi diffusers to prevent salt concentration pools.

Biological Purification

Wastewater Treatment and Biological Circular Reuse

Designing zero-emission Wastewater Treatment Plants (WWTPs) to restore urban outflows for agriculture and municipal reuse.

Through advanced tertiary separation processes and ultra-violet sanitization manifolds, we convert used municipal and agricultural water into nutrient-controlled reclaimed supplies. This biological recycling method decreases reliance on virgin ground sources, giving industrial hubs and farm networks a resilient local water supply.

Biological Nitrogen Removal

Utilizes custom anoxic chambers and aeration tanks to consume industrial carbon and ammonium complexes.

Membrane Bioreactors (MBR)

Integrates advanced micro-filtration layers directly into aeration tanks, replacing large settler ponds.

Thermal Sludge Drying

Treats suspended biomass through anaerobic digestion, synthesizing biogas used to power operational pumps.

Advanced UV Disinfection

Sanitizes biological residues without relying on chlorine additives, preventing downstream toxins.

Drinking Water Systems

Potabilization and Safe-Stream Infrastructure (DWTP)

End-to-end design, construction, and technical oversight of high-performance Drinking Water Treatment Plants.

We build customizable DWTP installations capable of treating diverse complex source materials (including high-turbidity rivers and mineralized aquifers). Modern treatment protocols merge physical coagulation, multi-stage ozonation, and active carbon adsorption to guarantee safe drinking water that satisfies strict EU and international standards.

Dynamic Coagulation Loops

Intelligent sensors adjust salt polymer infusions based on turbidity shifts, capturing microscopic solids.

Granular Active Carbon

Channels stream layers through carbon matrices to neutralize lingering tastes, traces, and pesticides.

Ozonation Chambers

Leverages high-reactivity ozone molecules to sanitize and oxidation-dissolve complex heavy compounds.

Pre-emptive Safety Alarms

Real-time telemetry networks report quality indices every 30 seconds to prevent supply line risks.

Smart Infrastructure

Intelligent Distribution Network Management

Direct operations, smart tele-metering, and professional customer care services for municipal networks.

Beyond physical infrastructure, we operate the active, day-to-day distribution layers of metropolitan utilities. Using intelligent telemetry networks, automated leak-detection algorithms, and consumer portals, we improve hydraulic performance and reduce water loss across vast distances.

Acoustic Tele-Leak Scans

Permanently placed pipe sensors listen for subtle structural vibrations to intercept silent pipeline fractures.

IoT Smart Meters

Encoders stream reading insights hourly, eliminating physical read margins and highlighting unusual consumption.

Dynamic Pressure Zones

Modulates mainline pump speeds during sleep hours to minimize mechanical wear and reduce stress points.

Omnichannel Helpdesks

Consumer web and mobile panels provide instant access to invoices, consumption data, and technician callouts.
